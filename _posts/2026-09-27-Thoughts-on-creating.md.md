### My thoughts on creating
Throughout my very short time on this earth, I have found that I enjoy creating. Whatever form it may take has changed drastically over time, but it has always been plagued by perfectionism. To combat that perfectionism, I created this blog to force myself to get comfortable publicizing work that does not meet my standards for perfect content. As an example of publishing imperfect content, here is where I’m supposed to craft a nice transition to the next paragraph. Unfortunately, my writing skills are lacking, and therefore you all must read this ugly mess. Now poof transition!!!

I’ve observed that a majority of my favorite content creators - in this instance, YouTubers- wouldn’t need video to properly convey their message; rather, it simply adds an extra medium to be creative with. While I would argue audio is also unnecessary, I’ve found it plays a much more influential role over the tone and impact of the content than video. (Please take a look at [The importance of a good soundtrack](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=muisc+changes+everything+star+wars) or similar videos for examples.) I came to this new view after watching Pengu’s video “[After 4 years with my endgame mouse, this made me swap…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=af8OZnhgAuc)” which is an excellent example of this type of content. Now how does this relate to my hopes of content creation? Over the coming months, I would like to produce as many quality blog posts as possible to practice my writing. Eventually I’ll switch over to videos that will be posted to my [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@MinimallyFunny), but I still expect each video to have an accompanying blog post, which will most likely be a 1-to-1 of the video script.
